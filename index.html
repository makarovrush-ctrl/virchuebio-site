Know what a result means

Thrombosis maps are exploratory model quantities under the inputs you enter. They are not calibrated patient risk estimates or a prediction that a clot will form, and the platform is not a medical device.

  • Your files stay with youGeometry and simulations are processed in your browser.

WHAT THE PLATFORM DOES

One workflow, from geometry to wall loading

Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop.
Save your case before closing the page.

Prepare the vessel

Import a fluid-lumen STL or a lumen with named caps, confirm its units, review every opening and assign inlet and outlet roles.

Simulate blood flow

Set flow and blood properties, generate a mesh with patient guidance and run a flow simulation with live progress and convergence checks.

Explore thrombosis maps

Compute the team's force-based thrombosis indices on the simulated flow, inspect them in 3D and export fields, figures and case records.

GET STARTED

One case, six steps

Patient-scale runs can take a while.
Keep the numerical status with any result you share.

  1. 01

    Load the fluid lumen

    Drop an STL, a lumen with named cap STLs, or its folder into vessel preparation, then confirm its physical units.

  2. 02

    Review every opening

    Check the detected openings, assign inlet or outlet roles, build the prepared geometry and select Use in ForgeFlow.

  3. 03

    Set the flow inputs

    Check mean inflow, density, viscosity and boundary conditions. Reference presets must be selected explicitly.

  4. 04

    Generate the mesh

    Follow the mesh guidance and inspect the geometry and openings. A successful mesh alone does not establish flow accuracy.

  5. 05

    Run and inspect flow

    Watch solver progress and convergence. Results from unfinished flow remain explicitly exploratory.

  6. 06

    Calculate and save

    Review the thrombosis inputs, run the calculation, inspect the 3D fields and export the case, results and feedback locally.
