From vessel geometry to thrombosis insight.

Prepare a vessel, simulate its blood flow and explore where the vessel wall is most exposed to clot-promoting forces. VirChue Bio X is a research platform from a University of Sydney team, free to use in your browser. Its rapid CFD module, ForgeFlow, solves the blood flow.

How it works

From a patient's vessel to its thrombogenic hotspots

ForgeFlow simulates how blood moves through the vessel. The FGT model turns those flow results into indexes such as the Thrombosis Number Θ, showing where clots could start under the conditions you set.

  1. 01

    Patient geometry

    Start from a patient's vessel: a fluid-lumen STL segmented from CT or MRI, with its inlet and outlets named during vessel preparation.

  2. 02

    Blood flows in

    Set the inflow and blood properties. Blood enters the vessel, speeds up through narrowings and slows into recirculation behind them.

  3. 03

    Rapid CFD with ForgeFlow

    ForgeFlow meshes the vessel and solves the blood flow, showing progress and convergence while it runs.

  4. 04

    FGT indexes such as Θ

    The flow results feed the FGT model, which maps indexes such as the Thrombosis Number Θ over the vessel wall and flags spots above the reference value Θ = 1 as possible thrombogenic spots under the given conditions.

The science

Built on Force-Gated Thrombosis

FGT is our theory of how blood flow forces can initiate a clot. It treats clot initiation as a force-gated transition and defines a dimensionless Thrombosis Number, Θ, that separates stable flow from clot growth. The platform applies it to patient-specific vessels.

Preprint

Liu X, Chen Y, Zhuang MS, Vigolo D, Yong K-T. Force-Gated Thrombosis (FGT): A Non-Equilibrium Mechanical Theory of Shear-Induced Blood Clot Initiation. bioRxiv, 2026. doi:10.64898/2026.05.17.725779

Know what a result means

Thrombosis maps are exploratory model quantities under the inputs you enter. They are not calibrated patient risk estimates or a prediction that a clot will form, and the platform is not a medical device.
