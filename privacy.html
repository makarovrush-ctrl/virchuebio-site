Privacy policy Effective 12 November 2026

Privacy policy.

How the VirChue Bio team handles information when you visit www.virchuebio.com, use the platform, or choose to contribute a case to research.

In short

  • Using the platform: your files and results are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.
  • Contributing a case: optional, case by case, and only with your consent. We keep contributed cases for research for up to 7 years, and you can withdraw them at any time.
  • We never sell information, show advertising or use tracking cookies.

Who we are

The platform is operated by the VirChue Bio team: Dr Mackenzie Siyuan Zhuang, Allan Sun and Professor Ken-Tye Yong ("we", "us"). Contact: contact@virchuebio.com. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you visit the website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type.

When you use the platform. Vessel geometry, settings and results are processed in your web browser on your own computer and are not sent to us. Exports and feedback files are saved to your computer.

When you contribute a case (optional). With your consent, we store the case's prepared vessel geometry, settings, key results and full 3D flow fields, together with your verified email address, the statements you agreed to, the date and a contribution ID. We do not collect original filenames, text inside file headers, DICOM images or tags, names, dates of birth, record numbers or clinical details, and you must not include them.

When you post in the forum or email us. Forum posts are public and handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement. If you email us, we keep your address and message to reply.

How we use it

  • to run the platform and its calculations, and to protect it from misuse;
  • to research thrombosis and blood flow, and to develop and improve the platform and its models, using cases you chose to contribute;
  • to contact you about a contribution, only if you agreed to this;
  • to answer your questions and requests.

We publish findings drawn from many cases only, never a contributed case on its own.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Contributed cases are stored encrypted in Google Cloud's Sydney region, in Australia. Only named members of the VirChue Bio team can open them, and access is logged. Your email address is stored separately from the case, linked only by a random contribution ID. We use service providers (Google Cloud for storage and calculations, GitHub for the website and forum) under their own terms. GitHub is based in the United States. We do not otherwise disclose your information unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Contributed cases: up to 7 years from contribution, then deleted, or sooner if you withdraw them.
  • Calculation server logs: 30 days.
  • Emails: as long as needed to deal with your request.

Your choices and rights

You can use the platform without contributing anything. You can withdraw a contribution at any time under "My contributions" in the platform, or by emailing us; we delete it within 30 days, although findings already published from many cases cannot be withdrawn. You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you. If you have a concern, contact us first; if you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Other points

The platform is intended for people aged 18 or over. If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above; changes do not apply to earlier contributions unless you agree.
