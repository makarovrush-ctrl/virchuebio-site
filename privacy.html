Privacy and terms of use.

By using the VirChue Bio platform you agree to these terms. Please read them before you use it with your own data.

Research use only. The platform is not a medical device. Its results are exploratory model outputs; they are not calibrated patient risk estimates and must not be used to diagnose, treat or make decisions about any person.

Your files and data

Vessel geometry, case settings and simulation results are processed in your web browser on your own computer. The platform has no upload service: your files are not sent to the team or stored on our servers.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives standard web request information such as your IP address and browser type. The community forum is hosted on GitHub Discussions; posts there are public and are covered by GitHub's own terms and privacy statement.

Exports and feedback files are saved to your computer, and nothing else is shared unless you choose to share it. Do not use files that identify a person: remove names, dates, record numbers and scan metadata before importing a geometry or sharing an export.
