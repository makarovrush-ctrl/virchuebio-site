Treatment planning — virtual stent

⚗ Research use only. Compares where a stent could sit on this geometry by the disturbed flow it leaves at its edges. It models the stent as a smooth tube: struts, malapposition, wall stretch, antiplatelet therapy and healing are not represented — the main real causes of stent thrombosis.
Follows the vessel carrying the fastest flow — the whole vessel if it doesn't branch — finds its narrowing, tries five placements that all cover it, and re-simulates each. Allow a few minutes.

Steady convergence requires both relative and absolute momentum change below the selected tolerance. Conservative momentum transport also requires the current steady-equation RMS below that tolerance. Absolute quantities use nondimensional acceleration (scale U²/L); continuity and pressure linear-solver diagnostics use different normalizations.

About the solver

Laminar, incompressible Navier–Stokes on a staggered Cartesian grid. Solid walls are represented by voxels (staircase), so accuracy depends on cells across the narrowest passage — aim for 10+. Verified against Poiseuille duct flow (2nd-order convergence) and Hagen–Poiseuille pipe flow (0.5 % at 20 cells per diameter).
