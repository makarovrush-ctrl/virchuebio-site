Community guidelines

  • Never post patient information. No names, dates of birth, record numbers, scan headers or anything else that could identify a person. Check filenames, screenshots and exported files before you attach them.
  • Research discussion only. The forum does not give medical advice, and results from the platform must not be used for clinical decisions.
  • Be specific and kind. Include what you did, what you expected and what happened. Respect other members and their work.
  • Posts are public. Anything you post can be read by anyone. For confidential matters, such as collaborations or unpublished data, contact the team instead.

The forum is hosted on GitHub Discussions, so GitHub's terms and privacy statement also apply to your posts. The team moderates the forum and may edit or remove posts that break these guidelines.
